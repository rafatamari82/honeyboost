Inside the formula

The 7 ingredients

Here's what each one is and how it has traditionally been used.

Tongkat Ali

A root from Southeast Asia, traditionally used in men's tonics. It is one of the more researched herbs in this category.

Horny Goat Weed

Epimedium, a leafy herb used for centuries in traditional Chinese practice for vitality.

Nettle

Stinging nettle root is a common herbal ingredient in men's formulas, often paired with saw palmetto.

Saw Palmetto

Berries from a palm native to the southeastern US, widely used in supplements aimed at men over 40.

Wild Yam

A climbing plant whose root has a long history in folk herbalism.

Sarsaparilla

A tropical vine root, historically used in tonics and, yes, the original root beer flavor.

Boron

A trace mineral found in nuts, fruit and vegetables, included in many men's wellness blends.

Exact amounts per serving are listed on the product label on the official site. Research on herbal ingredients is often limited or mixed, and results vary from person to person.
